Uninterrupted

A meditation timer for iPhone and Apple Watch

Uninterrupted is an idea I've had in my head for more than fifteen years, and I'm pretty excited to finally see it come to life.

The idea is simple: Just record how long you meditate.

Every meditation app is filled with gongs, ambient sound, or narration. Almost all of them require an account or a subscription to get started. Pretty much all bury the simple act of sitting down behind menus and settings.

Uninterrupted takes the opposite approach. It is a quiet, simple way to record your practice, however you practice. Tap Begin, meditate, and tap Done when you're finished. No sounds, no prompts, no interruptions.

Will this make money? Probably not, but that was never the goal.
